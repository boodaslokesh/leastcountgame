    How to Play Least Count

    1. Goal

    Keep the total points in your hand as low as possible. Players collect points when a round ends; reaching the game's elimination limit removes a player from later rounds. The last eligible player remaining wins.

    2. Starting a round

    Each active player receives the configured number of cards. A card is placed face-up to begin the discard pile, and the remaining cards form the stock deck. Only active, connected players who are allowed to play take turns.

    3. Taking your turn

    • Choose a card from your hand to discard. All cards in your hand with the same rank/value as the selected card are discarded together.
    • If that rank/value matches the top card already on the discard pile, your turn ends immediately and the next player goes.
    • Otherwise, draw one card from the stock deck. Your turn then ends and play moves to the next eligible player.
    • If the stock deck runs out while cards remain in the discard pile, the discard pile is recycled into the stock while its top card stays face-up.

    4. Round-robin first player

    The opening turn rotates by seat order from round to round: the host starts Round 1, the next eligible seat starts Round 2, then the following eligible seat starts Round 3, and so on. If the scheduled player is eliminated, disconnected, or view-only, the next eligible player is selected.

    Example with four seats: Round 1 — Player 1; Round 2 — Player 2; Round 3 — Player 3; Round 4 — Player 4; Round 5 — back to Player 1 (skipping any ineligible seat).

    5. Calling “Show”

    When your hand total is 5 points or less, you can use Call Show! (≤5) to end the round. The game compares the hand totals of eligible players.

    • Failed Show: if any other eligible player has a lower total than the caller, the caller receives a 25-point penalty.
    • Matched Show: if another eligible player has the same total as the caller and nobody has a lower total, the tied players avoid points for that round; other eligible players add their hand totals.
    • Good Show: if nobody has a lower or equal total, the caller avoids points and each other eligible player adds their hand total.

    6. Points and elimination

    For example, if a player has cards worth 1, 2, and 2 points, their hand total is 5. When a round result says that player adds their hand total, 5 points are added to their running score. A player is marked eliminated when their score reaches the configured elimination threshold. Eliminated players do not take turns in following rounds.

    7. Multiplayer and rejoining

    The host controls starting rounds. A view-only spectator can watch but cannot play cards or call Show. If you leave, use the room's rejoin process to request access again; the host can allow viewing, allow playing, or deny the request.

    Card point values and round settings follow the values configured in this game. Check the Scores and History panels to review totals and completed rounds.
